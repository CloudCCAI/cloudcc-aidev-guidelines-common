- Purpose: preserve and safely evolve the inventory availability calculation.
- Users: existing service callers and maintainers.
- In scope: documented compatibility, regression coverage, and small verified improvements.
- Out of scope: replacing the inventory domain or inventing undocumented production behavior.
- Success: every behavior change has caller evidence, an approved design, and a regression test.
